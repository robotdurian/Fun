# Sorting Hat — Simple Party Edition

**Before the party:** send guests these questions by any means (text, email, in person):
1. What should the Hat call you? 2. Favourite food? 3. Favourite animal? 4. What are you really good at? 5. What magic power would you pick?
6. A mysterious glowing door: fling it open / grab a friend first / read the clues / find a shortcut?
7. Someone is left out: invite them in loudly / sit with them / work out why and help / quietly fix it?
8. Friends should say you are: so brave / so kind / so clever / never give up?
Type each guest's answers into ⚙ → Guests. When a child says their name the Hat matches it (tolerates mishearing), shows the match on the house screen (change it with the Survey dropdown; ★ marks the house their choices lean towards) and weaves their answers into the musing, including "Keep musing" extras.

**Flow (audio-first, run from the host's phone, speaker out):**
1. Child puts on the hat. Host taps **Wake the Hat** (or **Next child**).
2. Hat asks the child's name, listens, and confirms ("Did I hear… Luna?"). Host can type the name silently as a fallback.
3. The host phone prompts **"Which house for Luna?"**. The host taps the house while the Hat says "keep very still while I think".
4. Hat muses and quips: cold-read observations, a tempting decoy house, a "hold on…" doubt, then hints at the real house.
5. **💭 Keep musing** (host): each tap queues an extra rambling segment (tangents, doubts, more house hints, never repeating) that plays after the current one, before the reveal. Stack as many as you like; it disappears once the reveal starts.
6. **⏭ Reveal now** (host): cuts the Hat off mid-sentence, clears any queued musing, and jumps straight to "Hm hm hm… I know exactly where to put you. Better be… HOUSE!"
7. **🔇 Too noisy** button (host): pauses the Hat mid-sentence ("I can't hear very well with all this noise"). Tap **▶ Quiet now** and it resumes ("Now, where was I?") from the interrupted line. Works during listening too.
8. "Better be… RAVENCLAW!", the house table updates, then a house dare and "Next wizard, step forward!"

**House table:** 2×2 cards of who is in which house with counts; tap a name to remove a mistake. Spoken scoreboard and Clear in ⚙.
**Setup:** serve over HTTPS (GitHub Pages / Netlify); Android + Chrome recommended; screen stays awake; ⚙ has voice picker.
Unofficial fan-made party game.
